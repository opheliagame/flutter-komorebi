# Kiritori (キリトリ) - Product Requirements Document

## Core Vision

A "second brain" for collecting and connecting ideas, balancing professional citation management (Zotero) with playful, low-stress exploration (MyMind). It emphasizes finding connections automatically and reorganizing unstructured thoughts (multimedia notes, text, images).

## Core Tenets: Privacy & Offline-First

**Offline-First**: Kiritori is a purely local application. No data is synced to the cloud.

**No Avatars/Accounts**: There are no user profiles, avatars, or login systems. The focus is entirely on the content.

**No Data Collection**: We do not collect user data. Privacy is built-in by design.

**No Premium Services**: All features are available locally without subscription tiers.

## Design Philosophy

**Calm Tech**: Soft, non-intrusive, and intuitive.

**Atmospheric Modes**: Day (bright), Twilight (golden hour), Night (deep evening sky).

**Tactile & Fluid**: "Saku-saku" (crisp/smooth) performance with purposeful animations.

**Visuals**: A mix of skeuomorphism, neomorphism, and flat design. Typography-centric.

**The Forest Metaphor**: A metaphor for interconnectedness and growth, not a literal visual constraint. Content should be diverse (politics, philosophy, body, freedom) and not strictly nature-focused.

## Key Features

**Multimodal Capture:** Text, images, citations, and "doors" (connections).

**Connection Web**: Focus on "trails" of thought.

**Dense Data Visualization**: Reducing negative space to prioritize visibility of actual notes and connections over abstract categories.

**Zoomable Interface**: Semantic zoom to view data from different scales.

**Roaming Mode**: Serendipitous exploration of existing notes.

**Analytics**: Visualizing the "ecology"—most used collections, media types, and activity frequency.

**Citation Management**: Local citation saving and export.

## Design Themes

**Colors**: Diverse palette (Green, Blue, Pink, Yellow, Earth tones) to reflect different subjects (e.g., Politics, Body, Freedom).

**Layout**: High information density; less "gallery" and more "archive."

**Typography**: Noto Serif for a grounded, academic yet approachable feel.
