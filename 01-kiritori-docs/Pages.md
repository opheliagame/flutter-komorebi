	current design does not work. there is no point in showing collections as a row on top because you cannot see a lot of them and it is not useful to have to scroll so much

bottom nav bar is good, but navigation should be tab navigation

data reports on top is generally a good idea

**Home page should feel like a welcoming door that encourages exploration**

interactive display of recently created connections with their similar connections, something like a soft venn diagram

get a zoomed out view of recent activity

sort of like roam page, but instead of being zoomed into a single note, explore at the level of collections
